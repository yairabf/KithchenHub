Delete your account and associated data

This page explains how FullHouse users can delete their account from inside the app, or request deletion by email if they cannot access the app.

Delete your account in the app

Open FullHouse, sign in, then follow these steps. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

1

Open Settings

Tap the Profile / Settings tab in the bottom navigation.

2

Scroll to Delete account

Scroll down in Settings and tap the Delete account row.

3

Confirm deletion

Read the confirmation message and tap Delete permanently.

If you cannot access the app

Email yair.solutions.19@gmail.com from the email address used for your FullHouse account and write: “Please delete my FullHouse account.”

If you cannot send from the account email, include the account email and enough information for us to verify ownership.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, FullHouse deletes or disassociates account data associated with your user account, including:

  • Account profile information, including name and email.
  • Household membership and invite-related data.
  • Shopping lists, grocery items, recipes, ingredients, chores, and other household content associated with your account where deletion is permitted.
  • Recipe images or uploaded content associated with your account where applicable.
  • Subscription/customer linkage associated with your app account where applicable.

Data we may retain

Some limited records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, payment, tax, or compliance reasons. For example, app store purchase records may remain with Google Play, Apple, or our subscription provider according to their policies.

Email deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days. Some backups or legally required records may remain for a limited additional period before being deleted or anonymized.
