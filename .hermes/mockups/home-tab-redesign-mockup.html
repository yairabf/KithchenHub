KitchenHub · Home tab redesign proposal

Action-first home tab with Quick Add + Frequently Added Items

This mockup removes the current Suggested Items block and the Saved Recipes / Shopping Lists stats row. The home tab becomes a cleaner launch surface focused on quick grocery input, fast repeat adds, and chores.

Removed: Suggested Items, Saved Recipes stat card, Shopping Lists stat card.
Added: a dedicated Frequently Added Items section directly under Quick Add, with larger tap targets and space for future personalization.
Behavior: tapping a frequent item should add/increment it immediately in the active list. If no list is active, keep the current fallback of opening the shopping modal.
